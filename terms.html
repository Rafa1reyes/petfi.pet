💖 The Petfi Pledge

Half of all Petfi profit — and every gift dollar Petfi receives, after the payment fee — goes back to animals

Half of all Petfi profit (store commissions, ads and the trust badge) and every gift dollar Petfi receives (what is left after Google Play's or Stripe's fee) go into the Pledge fund. Half of the fund helps ID-verified Petfi members: emergency veterinary bills when a family can't cover a sudden crisis, cremation costs after a sudden loss, pet food when money is tight, and adoption fees so shelter pets find their family faster. Members ask for help with a vet bill, a cremation or pet food by uploading the bill in the app; requests open once the fund reaches its opening goal, and help is never guaranteed. Petfi's treasurer decides each case, and members who have given the most are first in line. The other half goes to shelters and animal organizations — shelters, rescues, veterinary offices — to spend as they see fit. Every dollar given away is published on the Pledge page in the app (Me → The Petfi Pledge) with the date and the recipient; a member's own bill is listed by what it paid for, never by name. A live total of everything raised — every gift plus half of each month's profit, which Petfi enters once the month's expenses are known — is shown in the app and on petfi.pet. The Pledge page also lists real shelters near you to give to directly.

Community Guidelines

Petfi should feel like the friendliest dog park on the internet. Here's how we keep it that way.

💛 Be kind

Talk to people (and pets) like you're face to face at the park. No harassment, hate, or pile-ons — disagree nicely or scroll on.

🐶 Pets first

We love funny pet content. We never allow content showing animals hurt, scared, or neglected — report it the moment you see it.

🏡 Local vs 🌎 Everyone

Home has two feeds. Everyone is nationwide and family-friendly — posts and comments with slurs or profanity are blocked there automatically. Local shows posts from within about 50 miles of your ZIP and is unfiltered: talk like you would at the dog park. These guidelines still apply in both — no harassment, threats, hate, or anything illegal — and anyone can report a post. When you post you choose the feed; Local posts never appear in Everyone, and others see only your town, never your address.

🛒 Sell honestly

Real photos, real prices, real health info. If you're selling or breeding, disclose everything a caring buyer would want to know. Never sell homemade food or treats — consumables must be sealed, licensed products so no pet gets hurt.

🔒 Respect privacy

Don't share someone else's personal info, screenshots of private messages, or photos of people without permission.

🚩 See something? Report it

Every post and profile has a Report option. Reports are anonymous. You can also block anyone — they won't be able to message you or see you nearby.

What happens to rule-breakers

First offenses usually get content removed and a warning. Serious or repeated violations — especially anything involving animal welfare or scams — mean a permanent ban.
