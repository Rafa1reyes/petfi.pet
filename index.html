List your animal in about a minute: name, breed, male or female, age, terms. See who's nearby looking for the same thing, and message them. Dogs and cats — and goats, cattle, sheep, horses, pigs and poultry too.

Living pet profiles

Pick your species and breed and your pet gets a hand-drawn icon to match — dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, reptiles, fish, and now goats, cattle, sheep, horses, pigs and chickens by breed. Prefer the real thing? Use a photo, or let Petfi turn a photo of your pet into a cartoon icon.

Two feeds: Local & Everyone

Local is your area — about 50 miles around you, unfiltered, say it like you would at the dog park. Everyone is nationwide and family-friendly, with profanity filtered out automatically. Post photos or short videos, and react with real recorded barks & meows — every species has its own voice, moo, baa and cluck included.

💘 Mate Match

Find a breeding partner near you

Petfi was built by an owner with a great dog and no way to find him a female nearby. Mate Match fixes that — for pets and farm animals — and it's simple enough for anyone to use.

  • ➕List in about a minute: pick the animal, add the name, breed, male or female, age and your terms. Photos optional.
  • 📍Nearest first: listings sort by distance from your ZIP. Others see only your town and how far away you are — never your address.
  • 🎯Petfi tells you: the moment someone lists an animal that pairs with yours nearby, you get a heads-up. Message them right in the app.
  • 🐄Farm & ranch too: goats, cattle, sheep, horses, pigs, chickens, ducks, turkeys, alpacas — list a single animal or a whole herd.
  • 🧬Honest info up front: every listing shows age, health, papers and terms, so owners can talk responsibly first.

💖 The Petfi pledge

Half of all Petfi profit goes back to animals

Every purchase through the Petfi store, every ad and subscription helps pay adoption fees so shelter pets find their family faster and more of them make it out the door, emergency vet bills when a family can't cover a sudden crisis, and cremation costs after a sudden loss. Buying through Petfi costs you nothing extra. The app also finds real shelters near you so you can give directly.
