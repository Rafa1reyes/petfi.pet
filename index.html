💘 Mate Match

Find a breeding partner near you

Petfi started with one question: where do I find a mate for my dog? Its founder had a great dog and no way to find him a female nearby. Mate Match fixes that, for pets and farm animals.

  • ➕List in about a minute: pick the animal, add the name, breed, male or female, age and your terms. Photos optional.
  • 📍Nearest first: listings sort by distance from your ZIP. Others see only your town and how far away you are, never your address.
  • 🎯Petfi tells you: when someone lists an animal that pairs with yours nearby, you get a heads-up. Message them right in the app, and share a listing to your breed groups with one link.
  • 🐄Farm & ranch too: goats, cattle, sheep, horses, pigs, chickens, ducks, turkeys, alpacas. List a single animal or a whole herd.
  • 🪪Every breeder is ID-verified: listings come from real, verified adults and show age, health, papers and terms up front, so owners can talk responsibly first.

Safety first

Built for happy, healthy pets, and people

🧑

Verified where it matters

Every Petfi account is for adults 18 and older. Selling in the store, offering pet services and listing in Mate Match all require an Owner account and an ID check through Stripe Identity (a photo of your ID and a selfie, one-time $3). Petfi never sees the documents, and verified members get a trust badge.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Location is approximate, your ZIP is never shown to others, and we never sell your info. Block or report anyone in a tap; reports are reviewed by real moderators.

🐾

Animal welfare rules

No illegal or protected-animal sales, no homemade consumables, zero tolerance for abuse. Community guidelines everyone agrees to.
