💘 Mate Match

Matchmaking, with wagging tails

Whether you’re looking for a playdate, a companion, or a future litter, Mate Match pairs pets the thoughtful way.

  • 🐾Two paths: browse as your pet — or browse pets for your pet. Playdates or true romance; you set the intent.
  • 🔔Bark back: matches only happen when both sides bark back. No surprise suitors at the dog park.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑Humans stay in charge: owners chat first, meet safely, and decide what’s right for their pet.
