💖 The Petfi Pledge

Built to help animals, not to get rich

Petfi exists to help pets and the people who love them. So we made a promise and put it in writing: half of all Petfi profit, and every donation dollar Petfi receives after the payment fee, go back to animals.

50%of all Petfi profit, from store commissions, ads and the ID badge
100%of every donation dollar Petfi receives, after the payment fee
  • Half of the fund helps membersID-verified members can ask for help with an emergency vet bill, a cremation or pet food, and the fund covers adoption fees so shelter pets find a family faster. Petfi pays the vet, crematory or store directly. Members who give are first in line.
  • Half goes to shelters and animal organizationsShelters, rescues and vet offices receive the other half and decide how to use it.
  • Every dollar given away is publicEach gift from the fund is listed in the app with the date and who received it, so you can watch the money leave. A member's own bill is listed by what it paid for, never by name.

Safety and trust

A real, accountable person behind every listing

Anyone can join Petfi to post, make friends and use Lost & Found. But to offer anything to other members, you first have to verify your identity. That covers:

  • 🦮 Pet services
  • 🛒 Selling in the store
  • 💘 Mate Match listings
  • 📣 Spotlight ads
  • 🏢 Organization accounts
  • 🆘 Asking the Pledge for help

Petfi's own servers refuse a listing or an ad from an account that hasn't passed. It isn't just a button you can skip.

  1. A live photo of a government IDA driver's license, passport or ID card, photographed with the phone's camera on the spot. Uploading an old picture or a file is switched off.
  2. A live selfie, matched to the IDStripe compares the face in the selfie with the photo on the ID, and flags a selfie that looks edited.
  3. Stripe Identity checks the ID itselfIt looks for signs of a fake or altered document and checks that a real, physical ID was in front of the camera. The date of birth must show 18 or older.

Badges you can see

✓ Verified member Pet of a verified member

The blue check sits on everything a verified member posts, sells or lists. Their pets carry a blue paw.

What Petfi keeps

Only the result: passed or failed, and whether you're 18 or older. Your ID photos and selfie stay with Stripe, and deleting your account asks Stripe to erase them.

Accountability

Petfi keeps a record that each verification happened. If someone harms an animal or a person, or runs a scam, Petfi can remove their listings and close their account, and will cooperate with law enforcement as the law allows.

Report, block, remove

Every post, listing and profile can be reported, anonymously. Block anyone in a tap. Petfi reviews reports and can suspend or remove accounts. Repeated failed ID checks are limited too.

A sensible word: verification tells you a real, identified adult stands behind a listing. It isn't a background check, so still use your judgment: meet in a public place first, ask questions, and trust your instincts.

The ID check is a one-time $3, with the price shown before you pay. Petfi is for adults 18 and older.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is Petfi really free?

Yes. Joining and using Petfi is free, including Lost & Found, the QR ID tag, care reminders, the feeds, friends and groups.

A few things are optional and paid: the one-time $3 ID check (needed to sell, offer services, advertise or list in Mate Match), Spotlight ads, and gifts to the Pledge.

Do I need an Android phone or an iPhone?

Neither. Petfi runs in any web browser at petfi.pet, on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers. On Android you can also get it from Google Play. There is no App Store app yet, so iPhone users use the website.

Is my home address shown on Lost & Found?

No. A report shows the town, the landmark you type and an approximate distance, never your home address. People reach you by message inside Petfi, and a phone number appears only if you choose to add one.

How do you verify sellers and service providers?

Through Stripe Identity: a live photo of a government ID plus a live selfie that is matched to the photo on the ID, with checks for fake or altered documents and an 18+ check. Without it, Petfi's servers won't accept a listing, a service, an ad or a Mate Match post. Petfi receives only the result; the ID images stay with Stripe.

It confirms who someone is. It is not a background check, so meet in public and use your judgment.

Where does the money go?

Half of all Petfi profit, and every donation dollar Petfi receives after the payment fee, go into the Petfi Pledge fund. Half of the fund helps ID-verified members with vet bills, cremation, pet food and adoption fees; the other half goes to shelters and animal organizations. Every dollar given away is listed in the app with the date and the recipient. Help is never guaranteed, and gifts are not tax-deductible.

Who can join?

Adults 18 and older, with any kind of animal. One account holds you and all your pets, and Petfi works in English and Spanish.
